# Images Folder

This folder contains all the images used in your "All About Me" website.

## Required Images:

### Main Page (index.html):

- **profile.jpg** - Your profile photo for the hero section
- **about-me.jpg** - An additional photo of yourself for the about section
- **hometown1.jpg** - Photo of a landmark or scene from your hometown
- **hometown2.jpg** - Another hometown photo
- **hometown3.jpg** - Third hometown photo
- **hometown4.jpg** - Fourth hometown photo

### Hobbies Page (hobbies.html):

- **photography.jpg** - Image representing your photography hobby
- **reading.jpg** - Image of books or reading
- **cooking.jpg** - Image related to cooking or baking
- **music.jpg** - Image of musical instruments or music
- **sports.jpg** - Image related to sports or exercise
- **art.jpg** - Image of art supplies or artwork

### Interests Page (interests.html):

- **movies.jpg** - Image related to movies and TV shows
- **books-interest.jpg** - Different book-related image for interests
- **technology.jpg** - Image related to technology and coding
- **science.jpg** - Image related to science and nature
- **arts.jpg** - Image related to music and arts

## Image Guidelines:

1. **Size**: Recommended size is 800x600 pixels or larger
2. **Format**: Use .jpg, .png, or .webp formats
3. **Quality**: High-resolution images work best
4. **Content**: Choose images that represent you and your interests
5. **Rights**: Make sure you have permission to use all images

## Where to Find Images:

1. **Personal Photos**: Use your own photos when possible
2. **Stock Photos**: Use free stock photo websites like:
   - Unsplash.com
   - Pexels.com
   - Pixabay.com
3. **Creative Commons**: Search for Creative Commons licensed images
4. **School Resources**: Check if your school provides image resources

## Adding Images:

1. Save your images in this folder with the exact names listed above
2. The website will automatically display them
3. If an image doesn't load, check the filename matches exactly
4. Make sure images are not too large (under 2MB each is recommended)

## Placeholder Images:

Until you add your own images, the website will show placeholder images or broken image icons. This is normal and expected.
