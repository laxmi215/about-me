### 4. **Image Requirements**

- **Format:** JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Size:** At least 800x600 pixels recommended
- **File size:** Under 2MB each for fast loading

### 5. **Free Image Sources**

If you need more images, get them from:

- **Unsplash.com** - High-quality free photos

- **Pixabay.com** - Free images and photos
- Your own photos from your phone/camera

### 6. **Refresh Your Website**

After adding images, refresh your browser to see them appear!
